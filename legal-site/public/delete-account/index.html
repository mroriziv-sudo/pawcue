PawCue Privacy Policy

Delete your PawCue account and data

No sign-in is needed on this page. Either path below deletes everything PawCue holds about you and your dog.

The quickest way: in the app

Open PawCue → Settings → Account → Delete account and data.

Confirm, and it is done immediately. This works whether you use PawCue as a guest or signed in with Apple.

If you no longer have the app installed

Email pawcue.support@gmail.com from the address you used with Sign in with Apple (or the private relay address Apple gave you), with the subject line “Delete my PawCue account”. We will delete the account within 30 days and confirm by reply.

If you only ever used PawCue as a guest and have uninstalled it, the credential that identified your device is gone, and nothing links the remaining guest record to you. Reinstalling creates a fresh guest. If you still have the app installed, use the in-app path above before uninstalling.

What is deleted

  • Your dog’s profile
  • All training sessions, progress, streaks and plans
  • Your sign-in and the email address linked to it
  • Your identity with our subscription provider

What is kept

A record that a subscription transaction happened, with its store transaction identifiers, for as long as we are required to keep accounting records. It is no longer linked to you.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in your Apple ID or Google account subscription settings if you no longer want it.
