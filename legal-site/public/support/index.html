PawCue Privacy Policy

Support

PawCue is dog training in a few minutes a day: a free clicker, guided lessons and a daily plan for your dog.

Contact

Email pawcue.support@gmail.com for help, questions or feedback. We answer every message, usually within a few days.

Your subscription

PawCue Premium is billed by Apple or Google, not by us. To cancel, change or get a refund for a subscription, use your Apple ID or Google account subscription settings; a refund request goes to the store you bought from. If a purchase you made is not showing in the app, open Settings → Restore Purchases.

Your account and data

You can delete your account and every piece of data PawCue holds at any time, from inside the app or without it: delete your account. What we collect and why is in the Privacy Policy.
