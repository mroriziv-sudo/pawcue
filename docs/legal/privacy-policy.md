# PawCue Privacy Policy

**DRAFT — for review. Not yet published.**

_Effective date: [[EFFECTIVE_DATE]]_

PawCue is a dog-training app made by [[OPERATOR]] ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app
collects, why, where it is kept, who else handles it, and how you can delete it. It describes the app as it works
today. If the app starts collecting something new, this policy will be updated before it does.

## 1. The short version

- You can use PawCue without creating an account. A guest is a random identifier, not a name or an email.
- The app collects what it needs to do its job: a profile of your dog, your training activity, and — if you
  subscribe — your subscription status.
- We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone, health data or advertising identifiers, and we
  do not track you across other apps or websites.
- We do not sell your information and we do not show advertising.
- You can delete everything from inside the app (Settings → Account → Delete account and data), whether you
  are a guest or signed in.

## 2. What we collect

### 2.1 Identity

**Guest.** When you first open PawCue, the app creates an anonymous account for you on our servers. It is a
random identifier and a session credential stored securely on your device. It contains no name, email or phone
number. It exists so that your dog's profile and training history can be saved and, if you later sign in, moved
to your account.

**Sign in with Apple.** If you choose to sign in, Apple gives us an identifier for your Apple ID and an email
address. We ask Apple only for your email — not your name. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email", we receive a
private relay address instead of your real one; the app treats it the same way. We never see or store your
Apple password.

We do not offer email/password accounts and never store a password.

### 2.2 Your dog's profile

What you enter when you create a profile: your dog's name, birthdate, breed (optional), sex, and how many
minutes a day you want to train. Breed is stored for your own reference only; nothing in the app makes
predictions about a dog from its breed.

### 2.3 Training activity

To build your daily plan and show your progress, the app records the training you do: which lesson you started
or completed and when, how many steps and repetitions you logged, and which "not working?" troubleshooting
option you opened. From this it derives your progress and streaks. The plan the app generates for your dog is
also stored.

If you train while offline, this is kept on your device and sent to our servers when you are next online.

### 2.4 Subscriptions

If you subscribe to PawCue Premium, the purchase is made through Apple's App Store or Google Play, which handle
payment. We receive from them and from RevenueCat (§4) the information needed to know what you are entitled to:
the product you bought, transaction identifiers, the dates the subscription started, renews, expires or was
cancelled, and whether it was refunded. **We never receive your card or bank details.**

### 2.5 Kept only on your device

Your language, sound and haptics settings, and a cached copy of the lesson catalogue so the app works offline.
These never leave your device.

### 2.6 What we do not collect

- Location, contacts, calendar, photos or camera, microphone, motion or health data.
- Advertising identifiers or any cross-app tracking. The app shows no ads and includes no advertising or
  analytics software.
- Crash reports or usage analytics. No such service is integrated at this time.
- Anything processed by an artificial-intelligence service. No data leaves the app for any AI provider.

## 3. Why we use it

- To run the app: save your dog's profile, generate a training plan, resume where you left off, show progress.
- To keep your data with you: when you sign in, the training you did as a guest moves to your account so it
  follows you to another device.
- To honour your subscription: to check, server-side, whether Premium is active before unlocking a lesson.
- To help you if you write to us.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling for marketing, or automated decisions with legal effects.

## 5. How long we keep it

- **Dog profile, training activity, plans, progress, sign-in details:** for as long as your account (or guest
  identity) exists. Deleted immediately when you delete your account (§6).
- **Subscription and purchase records:** we keep a record that a transaction happened, with its store
  transaction identifiers, for as long as we are required to for accounting, tax and fraud-prevention purposes.
  When you delete your account, the link between those records and you is removed.
- **Guest data with no sign-in:** stays until you delete it from the app. If you uninstall the app without
  deleting your data first, the credential that gives access to it is gone from your device and the data can no
  longer be reached or recovered — by you or by us. Delete it from Settings before uninstalling if you want it
  removed from our servers.

## 6. Deleting your data

In the app: **Settings → Account → Delete account and data.** This works for guests and signed-in accounts
alike. It permanently deletes your dog's profile, all training history, progress, plans and your sign-in from our
servers, removes your identity from our subscription provider, and clears the app on your device. It cannot be
undone.

Without the app: [[WEBSITE]]/delete-account explains how to request deletion by email.

Deleting your account does **not** cancel a subscription. Subscriptions are managed in your Apple ID or Google
account settings; cancel there if you no longer want it.

## 7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access the personal information we hold about you, to
correct it, to delete it, to object to or restrict how we use it, to receive a copy of it, and to complain to a
supervisory authority. You can see and change your dog's profile in the app, delete everything as described in
§6, and ask us for anything else at [[CONTACT_EMAIL]]. We will respond within the time the applicable law
allows.

Users in Israel: this policy is provided in accordance with the Protection of Privacy Law, 5741-1981. You are
not legally required to provide any of the information described above; without it, the corresponding features
of the app will not work.

## 8. Security

Data travels only over encrypted connections (TLS). Sign-in credentials on your device are kept in the operating
system's secure storage (Keychain on iOS, Keystore on Android). On our servers, every table is protected by
row-level access rules so that one user's data is never readable by another, and subscription status is decided
only by our server, never by the app on your device.

## 9. Children

PawCue is for dog owners and is not directed to children under 13 (or the age at which consent is required
where you live). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has
provided us with personal information, contact us at [[CONTACT_EMAIL]] and we will delete it.

## 10. Changes to this policy

When we change this policy we will update the effective date above and, for material changes, tell you in the
app before the change takes effect.
