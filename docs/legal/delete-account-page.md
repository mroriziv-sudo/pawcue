# Delete your PawCue account and data

**DRAFT — for review. To be published at [[WEBSITE]]/delete-account.**

Google Play requires a public, login-free web page where a user can request account deletion without the app
installed, linked from the Data Safety form. This page must go straight to the request flow — it is not a
marketing page. Apple reviewers also appreciate finding the same path.

---

## The quickest way: in the app

Open PawCue → **Settings → Account → Delete account and data**.

Confirm, and it is done immediately. This works whether you use PawCue as a guest or signed in with Apple.

## If you no longer have the app installed

Email **[[CONTACT_EMAIL]]** from the address you used with Sign in with Apple (or the private-relay address Apple
gave you), with the subject line "Delete my PawCue account". We will delete the account within 30 days and confirm
by reply.

If you only ever used PawCue as a guest and have uninstalled it, the credential that identified your device is
gone, and there is nothing that links the remaining guest record to you. Reinstalling creates a fresh guest. If
you still have the app installed, use the in-app path above before uninstalling.

## What is deleted

- Your dog's profile
- All training sessions, progress, streaks and plans
- Your sign-in and the email address linked to it
- Your identity with our subscription provider

## What is kept

A record that a subscription transaction happened, with its store transaction identifiers, for as long as we are
required to keep accounting records. It is no longer linked to you.

## Subscriptions

Deleting your account does **not** cancel a subscription. Cancel it in your Apple ID or Google account
subscription settings if you no longer want it.
